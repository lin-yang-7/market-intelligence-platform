# Project Overview

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

## 1. Project Overview

Market Intelligence Platform is an API-first cryptocurrency market intelligence platform designed for professional traders, quantitative teams, developers, and institutions.

The platform aggregates market data from multiple exchanges, computes quantitative features, generates rankings and trading signals, and exposes standardized REST and WebSocket APIs.

It is designed as infrastructure rather than a trading platform.

## 2. Vision

Build a scalable and extensible intelligence platform capable of serving dashboards, third-party applications, SDKs, and future AI models.

## 3. Product Goals

- Aggregate multi-exchange market data
- Compute reusable quantitative features
- Generate intelligent rankings and signals
- Provide stable public APIs
- Support historical analytics
- Prepare for AI integration
